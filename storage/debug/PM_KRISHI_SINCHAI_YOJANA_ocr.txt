Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY) was launched on 1st July 2015 with the motto of
‘Har Khet ko Pani‘ for providing end to end solutions in irrigation supply chain, viz., water
resources, distribution network, farm level applications and improving water use efficiency. Micro
irrigation (MI) is being popularized to ensure ‘Per Drop - More Crop’ (PDMC).
